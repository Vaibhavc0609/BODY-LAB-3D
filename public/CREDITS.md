# Medical 3D Model Credits & Data Attributions

All anatomical 3D assets in this project are 100% open-access, scientific, and peer-reviewed medical datasets:

### 1. Z-Anatomy Open Human Atlas (`z-anatomy-body.glb`)
- **Source**: Z-Anatomy Open Source 3D Human Anatomy Atlas (https://www.z-anatomy.net / https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy)
- **License**: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)
- **Coverage**: Complete human body containing 826 selectable anatomical structures and 387 muscles mapped to Terminologia Anatomica (TA2) standards.

### 2. DBCLS BodyParts3D 4.3 Complete Skeleton (`bodyparts3d-skeleton.glb`)
- **Source**: Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS), Japan (https://lifesciencedb.jp/bp3d/) / OrganUI (https://github.com/organui/skeleton-3d)
- **License**: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.1 Japan (CC BY-SA 2.1 JP)
- **Coverage**: 242 individually segmented bones across neurocranium, viscerocranium, vertebral column, thoracic cage, pelvic girdle, and extremities.

### 3. NIH Human Reference Atlas (HuBMAP) & Allen Brain Institute (`hra-*.glb`)
- **Source**: NIH Human BioMolecular Atlas Program (HuBMAP) 3D Reference Organ Library (https://humanatlas.io/3d-reference-library) & Allen Institute for Brain Science (https://atlas.brain-map.org/)
- **License**: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
- **Coverage**: 14 high-fidelity clinical visceral models:
  - Heart (with mitral, tricuspid, aortic, pulmonary valves, ventricles, atria, and papillary muscles)
  - Brain (Allen Institute whole encephalon with 283 anatomical sub-structures)
  - Lungs, Liver, Kidneys, Spleen, Pancreas, Colon, Small Intestine, Full Vasculature, Bladder, Eye, Larynx, Uterus.

*Note: For interactive visualization, education, and research exploration.*
